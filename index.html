Safety is a habit, not a poster on the wall.

I was born and raised in Qatar and have worked at Hamad Medical Corporation since 2019. I am completing a BEng (Hons) in International Fire Safety Engineering with the University of Central Lancashire at Ras Laffan Emergency and Safety College, and volunteer with HMC Fire & Safety across HMC hospitals.

My final-year dissertation used fire modelling (FDS) to study how door and window openings change smoke, heat and visibility in a room fire, and how long people have to get out safely.
